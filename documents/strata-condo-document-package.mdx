Condo financing can involve both your own income documents and information about the building. In BC, strata documents have specific names and purposes.

## Why the lender asks for it

The lender may review strata fees, insurance and property-related issues. Your realtor and legal adviser review other risks; mortgage review is not a substitute for their due diligence.

## How to obtain it

1.  Ask your realtor what the lender, purchase contract and legal professional each require. The requested package may include a Form B, budget, financials, meeting minutes, depreciation report and insurance information.
2.  In BC, request the Form B Information Certificate through the strata corporation or property manager, usually coordinated by the realtor. Ask that the issued certificate and its required attachments be provided together.
3.  For other strata records, the owner or someone with the owner’s written authorization can request access. A prospective buyer should arrange the correct authorization rather than assume unrestricted access.
4.  Outside BC, ask the realtor or lawyer for the province’s equivalent condo-document process. Ontario status certificates and BC Form B certificates are not the same document.

## Before you upload

- Confirm the records refer to the correct strata lot and corporation.
- Provide all requested attachments and disclose fee changes or special levies identified in the records.
- Check whether an updated certificate or insurance document is requested before closing.

## Common mix-ups

BC Form F is a Certificate of Payment used in the transfer process; it is not the Form B information package. A listing’s strata-fee figure is not a substitute for official documents. Retrieval fees, authorization and timing can vary.

## If you are stuck

If the property is self-managed or records are delayed, tell your realtor and Matt early. Let the legal professional explain your contractual rights and deadlines.

Upload requested records through your application portal. Do not share your CRA or banking password, PIN, security answers or one-time codes. Ask Matt before redacting sensitive details; do not change figures, dates or transactions.
