The lender needs to understand both where your down payment is held and where it came from. A balance screenshot rarely tells the full story.

## Why the lender asks for it

Complete account history helps verify ownership, availability and the source of deposits. Moving money between your own accounts does not remove the need to explain its origin.

## How to obtain it

1.  Open your bank or investment provider’s statements or documents section and download issued statements for the requested period. Lenders often request roughly three months, but your actual request controls.
2.  If the account is new or the statement cycle leaves a gap, ask the institution for a current transaction record or supporting letter. Confirm with Matt which format is acceptable.
3.  For transfers, obtain the sending-account record, transfer confirmation and receiving-account record. Include investment-sale records, gift evidence or sale proceeds where those supplied the funds.
4.  Tell Matt early about overseas funds, cash deposits, borrowed funds or large unusual deposits; translation or additional source records may be requested.

## Before you upload

- Include account-holder names, identifying account details, dates, balances and all issued pages.
- Make the trail continuous, including money that arrived from another institution.
- Keep enough funds available for closing costs as well as the down payment.

## Common mix-ups

A cropped balance screenshot does not show source history. There is no universal dollar threshold below which deposits never need explaining. Gift letters, investment balances and transfer receipts each support different parts of the trail.

## If you are stuck

If statements do not show your name or an account has closed, ask the institution for official copies and tell Matt. Do not recreate a statement or remove unrelated transactions yourself.

Upload requested records through your application portal. Do not share your CRA or banking password, PIN, security answers or one-time codes. Ask Matt before redacting sensitive details; do not change figures, dates or transactions.
