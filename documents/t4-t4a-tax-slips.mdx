A T4 reports employment remuneration. A T4A reports certain other amounts, such as pension income or some payments from other issuers; it is not always an employment slip.

## Why the lender asks for it

Tax slips help establish the amount and type of income reported by each employer or payer. They may support an income history but do not by themselves confirm your current employment.

## How to obtain it

1.  Check your employer’s payroll portal or the payer’s document service for the requested year. Download the complete issued slip.
2.  If it is missing, request a replacement from payroll, HR or the organization that issued it. For a T4A, that organization may not be your employer.
3.  You can also check available tax slips in your CRA Individual account. Choose the requested year. CRA can only show a slip after the issuer has supplied it.

## Before you upload

- Provide slips for each relevant employer or payer, not only the largest one.
- Match the tax year to the request and disclose amended slips.
- Keep identifying information and income boxes legible; ask about permitted SIN redaction.

## Common mix-ups

A pay stub is current payroll evidence, while a T4 summarizes a tax year. A T4A, T4E and T5 report different income sources. Do not assume that every amount on a slip qualifies for mortgage purposes.
