A Notice of Assessment (NOA) is the summary CRA issues after assessing your personal income tax return. You may also receive a Notice of Reassessment (NOR) if the assessment changes.

## Why the lender asks for it

It helps verify assessed income and tax information for the requested year. It is different from your filed tax return and from proof of your current tax-account balance.

## How to obtain it

1.  Start at the official Canada.ca CRA sign-in page and sign in to your own account. Open your Individual account, then Tax returns.
2.  Choose the year requested and open the Notice of Assessment or Notice of Reassessment. View or print the complete notice; save a PDF using the available download or print option.
3.  Repeat for each requested year. If online access is unavailable, use CRA’s official instructions to request a mailed copy. Tell Matt if the return has not yet been assessed.

## Before you upload

- Include every page, your name and the tax year.
- If a reassessment exists, tell Matt and provide the latest notice for that year as requested.
- Check the year carefully: a notice issued this year may relate to last year’s income.

## Common mix-ups

A T4, a return-summary screen and a proof of income statement are not the same as an NOA. An old notice showing no amount owing does not establish today’s tax balance.

## If you are stuck

Let Matt know whether you cannot sign in, cannot find the year or are waiting for an assessment. Do not delay telling us while trying to solve CRA access on your own.

Upload requested records through your application portal. Do not share your CRA or banking password, PIN, security answers or one-time codes. Ask Matt before redacting sensitive details; do not change figures, dates or transactions.
