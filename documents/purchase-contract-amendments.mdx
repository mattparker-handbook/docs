Once you have an accepted offer, the lender needs the actual agreement—not just a listing or a photo of the price page.

## Why the lender asks for it

The contract identifies the property, buyers, price, deposits, conditions and completion dates. Changes can affect the mortgage approval, funding arrangements or required documents.

## How to obtain it

1.  Ask your realtor, lawyer or notary for the complete accepted purchase agreement with all schedules and addenda. If you bought privately, obtain the complete signed agreement from the legal professional assisting you.
2.  Download the final signed PDF from the signing service or request a complete copy. Check that required signatures and acceptance information are present.
3.  Upload each later signed amendment or addendum requested, including changes to price, closing dates, purchaser names, credits or conditions. Provide subject-removal documents when requested.
4.  For a presale or assignment, tell Matt at the start. Developer contracts, disclosure documents, assignment agreements and legal review can require a different package.

## Before you upload

- Include every contract page and the schedules it references.
- Make sure the property and purchaser names match the mortgage application, and flag differences.
- Share financing and completion deadlines promptly; do not wait for all other documents to be ready.

## Common mix-ups

An MLS listing is property information, not the purchase agreement. An unsigned draft or text message about a change does not replace the appropriate signed record. Mortgage review alone is not permission to waive financing conditions.

## If you are stuck

Ask Matt to identify the missing page or document so your realtor can supply the right version. Seek legal advice before changing obligations or removing conditions.

Upload requested records through your application portal. Do not share your CRA or banking password, PIN, security answers or one-time codes. Ask Matt before redacting sensitive details; do not change figures, dates or transactions.
