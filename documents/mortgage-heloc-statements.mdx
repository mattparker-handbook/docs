For a renewal, refinance or purchase while owning another property, the lender may ask for statements for your existing mortgage and home equity line of credit (HELOC).

## Why the lender asks for it

These records help verify balances, payments and the debts secured against each property. A HELOC can be separate from, or part of, a combined lending arrangement.

## How to obtain it

1.  Sign in to your current lender’s account service and download the mortgage statement for the requested period. Look separately for any HELOC or other secured-loan statements.
2.  If you cannot access the document, contact mortgage servicing or your branch and request an official statement showing borrower names, the property, balance, payment and maturity details where available.
3.  If an exact payout is needed, coordinate with Matt and your lawyer or notary. The current lender must issue the appropriate dated payout information; do not estimate it from your account balance.

## Before you upload

- Match each mortgage and HELOC to the correct property.
- Include all pages and tell Matt about additional secured borrowing.
- Provide a renewal offer separately if requested; an offer does not replace every statement.

## Common mix-ups

A regular statement balance may omit accrued interest, penalties or discharge costs included in a payout. A payout quote can be date-sensitive. A property-tax notice is a separate record.

## If you are stuck

If the account is joint, paid off recently or appears under a different name, let Matt know and ask the lender for the right confirmation.

Upload requested records through your application portal. Do not share your CRA or banking password, PIN, security answers or one-time codes. Ask Matt before redacting sensitive details; do not change figures, dates or transactions.
