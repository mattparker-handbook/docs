CRA’s proof of income statement summarizes assessed income and deductions for a tax year. You may hear it called an “Option C” printout.

## Why the lender asks for it

It can help verify assessed income when the lender specifically accepts or requests it. It is not automatically interchangeable with an NOA or the complete T1 return.

## How to obtain it

1.  Sign in to your CRA account and open your Individual account. Go to Tax returns, then Proof of income statement.
2.  Select the requested year and view or print the statement. Save a complete, readable PDF where your device allows.
3.  If you cannot use the online service, follow CRA’s official telephone instructions to request a mailed statement. Arrange this early rather than relying on arrival before your financing deadline.

## Before you upload

- Confirm the requested year and include the full statement.
- This document displays your SIN: use your application portal, and ask before redacting it.
- Check with Matt that this is the exact document being requested before replacing another CRA record.

## Common mix-ups

It is a summary of assessed tax information, not a statement of current payroll or an employer letter. It also does not necessarily show that a current tax debt has been cleared.

## If you are stuck

If you are unsure whether the request means an NOA or proof of income statement, send Matt the request wording rather than ordering both unnecessarily.

Upload requested records through your application portal. Do not share your CRA or banking password, PIN, security answers or one-time codes. Ask Matt before redacting sensitive details; do not change figures, dates or transactions.
