Getting a mortgage in BC involves more moving parts than most buyers expect — lenders, appraisers, lawyers or notaries, realtors, and government offices all play a role at different stages. This page maps the entire process from your first financial conversation to closing day, so you always know what's happening, who's responsible, and how long each stage typically takes. Bookmark it and check back as your purchase progresses.

## Full Mortgage Timeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Financial Assessment">
    **What happens:** Your mortgage broker reviews your income, debts, credit profile, and savings to determine what you can realistically borrow and what mortgage products you may qualify for. This is a no-obligation conversation — nothing is submitted to a lender yet.

    **Who is involved:** You and your mortgage broker.

    **How long it takes:** Typically completed in a single meeting or call of 30–60 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Document Collection">
    **What happens:** Your broker provides a personalized document checklist based on your employment type and financial situation. You gather items such as pay stubs, T4s or NOAs, bank statements, and government-issued ID. The completeness of your package directly affects how quickly a pre-approval can be issued.

    **Who is involved:** You and your mortgage broker.

    **How long it takes:** 1–5 business days depending on how quickly documents can be located and uploaded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pre-Approval Application">
    **What happens:** Your broker submits your financial information to one or more lenders for a pre-approval. The lender conducts a credit check, reviews your documents, and issues a pre-approval certificate confirming your maximum borrowing amount and a rate hold (usually 90–120 days).

    **Who is involved:** You, your mortgage broker, and the lender's underwriting team.

    **How long it takes:** 1–3 business days for most straightforward applications; longer for complex income or credit situations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Property Search">
    **What happens:** Working with your realtor, you view properties within your pre-approved budget. Your broker remains available to answer questions about how specific properties (e.g., condos, strata units, rural properties) may affect your mortgage qualification.

    **Who is involved:** You, your realtor, and your mortgage broker (on an as-needed basis).

    **How long it takes:** Varies widely — from a few days to several months depending on the market and your criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subject-to-Financing Offer">
    **What happens:** Once you find a property, your realtor submits a purchase offer that includes a **subject to financing** clause. This clause protects you by giving a defined number of business days to confirm your mortgage before the deal becomes firm. The offer will also specify the purchase price, completion date, and possession date.

    **Who is involved:** You, your realtor, and the seller (via their realtor).

    **How long it takes:** Offer negotiation typically resolves within 24–72 hours. The subject removal period is then set contractually — usually 3–7 business days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Full Mortgage Application">
    **What happens:** With an accepted offer in hand, your broker submits a complete mortgage application to the chosen lender. This includes the accepted offer, property details (MLS listing, address), and your full document package. The lender assigns an underwriter to review the file.

    **Who is involved:** You, your mortgage broker, and the lender's underwriting team.

    **How long it takes:** Submission to underwriting review typically takes 1–3 business days; complex files or high-volume periods may take longer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Appraisal & Conditions">
    **What happens:** The lender may require an independent appraisal to confirm the property's market value supports the purchase price. Additional conditions may include a satisfactory home inspection, updated employment confirmation, or strata documents for condo purchases. Your broker coordinates with the lender and advises you on each condition.

    **Who is involved:** You, your mortgage broker, the lender, and a certified appraiser (if required).

    **How long it takes:** Appraisals typically take 2–5 business days to schedule and report. Satisfying all lender conditions usually takes 3–7 business days total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commitment Letter">
    **What happens:** Once all conditions are satisfied, the lender issues a **mortgage commitment letter** confirming the approved loan amount, interest rate, term, amortization, and any remaining conditions. You review and accept the commitment, and your broker walks you through every term before you sign.

    **Who is involved:** You, your mortgage broker, and the lender.

    **How long it takes:** Commitment letters are typically issued within 1–2 business days of all conditions being cleared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lawyer or Notary Instructions">
    **What happens:** Your broker forwards mortgage instructions to your chosen BC lawyer or notary public. They prepare the mortgage documentation, conduct a title search, arrange title insurance, and calculate your final closing costs and adjustments. You'll attend a signing appointment before the completion date.

    **Who is involved:** You, your lawyer or notary, and the lender's legal team.

    **How long it takes:** Instructions are sent 1–2 weeks before closing. Your signing appointment is typically 5–10 days before completion. Allow 1–2 hours for the signing meeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Closing Day">
    **What happens:** On the **completion date**, the lender advances mortgage funds to your lawyer or notary, who registers the title transfer with the BC Land Title and Survey Authority (LTSA). Property Transfer Tax and all closing costs are paid from your funds on deposit. On the **possession date** (often the same day or one day later), you receive the keys.

    **Who is involved:** Your lawyer or notary, the lender, the seller's lawyer or notary, and the LTSA.

    **How long it takes:** Title registration and fund transfer usually complete by mid-afternoon. Key handover timing is coordinated between your realtor and the seller's realtor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Typical BC Mortgage Timeline

| Stage | Typical Duration | Key Actions |
|---|---|---|
| Financial Assessment | 1 day | Initial broker meeting, budget discussion |
| Document Collection | 1–5 business days | Gather income, ID, and asset documents |
| Pre-Approval | 1–3 business days | Credit check, lender submission, rate hold |
| Property Search | Days to months | Viewings, market analysis with realtor |
| Offer & Acceptance | 1–3 days | Draft offer, negotiate terms, subject clause |
| Subject Period (Financing) | 3–7 business days | Full application, appraisal, condition review |
| Commitment Letter | 1–2 business days | Lender issues formal approval |
| Lawyer/Notary Preparation | 1–2 weeks before closing | Title search, document prep, signing appointment |
| Closing & Possession | Completion day | Funds advanced, title registered, keys received |

<Warning>
  **Subject removal deadlines are firm in BC.** If you do not remove your financing subject by the agreed date and time, the seller has the right to void the contract. Always confirm your subject removal deadline in writing with your realtor and keep your broker informed of any delays in document collection or appraisal scheduling. Build in buffer time — do not wait until the final day to communicate with your broker.
</Warning>

<Info>
  **BC uses both lawyers and notaries for property conveyancing.** Unlike some provinces where only lawyers handle real estate closings, BC notaries public are fully authorized to complete residential property transfers, register mortgages, and handle title insurance. Notaries often charge slightly less than lawyers for straightforward purchases. If your transaction involves complex legal issues — such as title disputes, estate matters, or commercial elements — a real estate lawyer is recommended. Your broker can provide referrals to trusted conveyancing professionals throughout BC.
</Info>
