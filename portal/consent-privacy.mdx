## Read the agreement before agreeing

Before submitting the online application, review the Credit Consent Agreement shown in your application. It explains the authorizations being requested. If you are uncertain about a credit check, information sharing or how long consent applies, ask before checking the consent box.

Consent to review a mortgage application is not a promise of approval. It is also not automatically consent to receive every type of marketing message. Follow the actual agreements you are presented with.

## Why identification is requested

Mortgage professionals and other parties may have legal identity-verification obligations. Lenders and lawyers/notaries also have their own identity requirements. An uploaded copy can support the process, but does not necessarily complete verification. An in-person or approved remote verification step may still be required.

## SINs and sensitive details

Do not send your SIN in a general email or support chat. If requested, ask why it is needed, whether it is required for that purpose and how to provide it securely. Government-issued ID, a CRA document and bank records can contain information that should not be casually shared.

Do not redact or cover parts of requested mortgage records without checking first: removing details can make the document unusable. Equally, do not send unrelated sensitive records just because you think they might help. Ask what is actually required.

## Protect access

Use a unique password and your own device where possible. Keep invitations private. Never give our team your bank login, password, PIN or one-time code. If an optional bank-connection feature appears in your portal, read its own consent and privacy information; using it is not a substitute for understanding the permission you are granting. Ask us about a manual-statement alternative.

## Beware of last-minute payment scams

A mortgage transaction can attract impersonation attempts. Treat unexpected instructions to move money or a change in bank details as a reason to pause. Verify with your lawyer/notary using a trusted phone number, not a new number in the message. Do not make a transfer based only on an email, text or chat.
