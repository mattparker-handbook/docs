## One mortgage, individual responsibilities

A co-borrower is someone applying for the mortgage with you. Each person’s income, debts, identity and circumstances may be relevant. Before adding someone, discuss with us whether they will be a borrower, a guarantor, an owner, a gift donor or simply helping you organize paperwork. These roles are not interchangeable.

## Adding a co-borrower

The application allows a borrower to add a co-borrower, or our team can help. Use each person’s legal name and correct details. A separate email address is needed when documents are sent to that person for their own electronic signature.

Once a co-borrower is listed, a borrower with an account can open **Documents**, select that co-borrower’s name and upload the requested files for them. Check the name carefully before uploading: one person’s pay stub must not be filed as the other person’s income.

## Uploading is not signing

Helping someone upload does not authorize you to consent, sign or impersonate them. Follow each consent or signature request as addressed. Ask us if a borrower cannot access their email or needs an alternative signing arrangement.

## Shared access and privacy

The portal supports borrowers uploading documents for other borrowers on the same application. Do not assume the application is a private space between you and Matt that other applicants cannot access. If there are sensitive finances, a separation, a guarantor relationship or information you do not want another applicant to see, speak with us privately before adding it. We can clarify the available access arrangements; do not assume a hide/private setting exists.

Do not share one person’s login to avoid setting up another account. A separate email and appropriate account setup help avoid signing and identity errors.

## Make collaboration easier

Choose who will gather shared property documents and down-payment history. Keep each person’s income and ID files clearly named. Tell us who should receive routine updates while recognizing that required consents and legal responsibilities remain individual.
