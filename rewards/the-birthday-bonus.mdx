## What is it?

Add your birthday to your profile and **10 bonus points (\$10)** land in your Rewards Bank every year on your birthday — our treat.

## How do I add my birthday?

Open your rewards portal, go to the **Profile** tab, and add your birthday. That's it — the bonus is automatic every year after that.

## When do the points arrive?

On your birthday. You'll also get a birthday email from Matt letting you know the points have been deposited.

## Anything else?

Keep an eye on your mailbox — Matt also sends a signed birthday card to members, which includes a little something extra inside.
