## How do I earn points?

You earn **10 points (\$10)** the moment Matt connects with someone you introduce, and another **90 points (\$90)** when their mortgage funds — **100 points total for every funded referral**. Every point is worth \$1 CAD.

## What counts as a referral?

Anyone you introduce to Matt who could use mortgage help — buying, renewing, refinancing or investing. Submit them through the **Submit a Referral** button in your rewards portal and Matt takes it from there. Make sure they're expecting his call.

## When do the 10 intro points arrive?

The 10 intro points are awarded when Matt has actually met and connected with your referral — not the moment you send the introduction. This keeps things fair: points are for real connections, not just names.

## What if a referral doesn't work out?

If a deal is cancelled after Matt connected with your referral, you keep the 10 intro points — the 90 funded points simply don't apply. Cancelled deals don't count toward milestone bonuses.

## Other ways to earn

- **Birthday bonus:** add your birthday to your profile and 10 bonus points land in your Rewards Bank every year on your birthday.
- **Monthly draw:** the monthly winner gets 100 points added straight to their balance.
- **Spin-the-wheel bonus:** after each monthly draw, spin the reveal wheel in your portal — even if you didn't win, you get +1 bonus entry in the next draw just for checking.

## Is there a limit?

No. There's no cap on referrals, points, or how much you can earn in a year.
