When your referrals fund, you unlock cash bonuses on top of the points you already earned:

| Funded referrals | Tier         | Bonus |
|------------------|--------------|-------|
| 3                | Pocket Money | \$100 |
| 5                | Big Bucks    | \$250 |
| 10               | Money Legend | \$500 |

## How milestones work

- Only **funded** referrals count — introductions alone don't move the counter.
- Cancelled deals don't count toward milestones.
- Bonuses are paid as points (1 point = \$1 CAD) added to your Rewards Bank.
- Each tier you unlock also adds a **permanent bonus entry to every monthly draw — forever**. Reach Pocket Money and you get +1 entry every month; reach Money Legend and you get +3.

## Where do I see my progress?

Your rewards portal shows your funded referral count and how many deals away you are from the next tier. Your monthly email update also includes your progress toward the next milestone.
