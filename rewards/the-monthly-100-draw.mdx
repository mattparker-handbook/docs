## How does it work?

Every member gets **one free entry each month**, plus **one extra entry for every referral you send that month**. The draw runs automatically on the last day of the month. The winner gets **100 points (\$100)** added straight to their Rewards Bank — redeem anytime or keep stacking.

## Do my entries roll over?

No — monthly entries reset on the 1st, so every member starts fresh each month. The exception: **milestone bonus entries** (earned by unlocking milestone tiers) stay with you every month, permanently.

## How do I find out if I won?

As soon as the monthly winner is drawn, a **spin wheel** appears at the top of your Rewards Bank for 7 days. Spin it to reveal whether you won. Just for spinning, you get **+1 bonus entry in next month's draw** — even if it wasn't your month.

## Do I need to do anything to enter?

No. Every active member is entered automatically — there's no opt-in checkbox and nothing to fill out.

## Is the draw fair?

Yes. The winner is picked randomly from all entries, and the more referrals you've sent that month (plus your milestone bonus entries), the more chances you have.
