## How do I log in?

There's no password to remember. Your personal portal link is emailed to you — click it and you're in. You can also request a fresh link anytime from [rewards.mattparker.ca](https://rewards.mattparker.ca).

## How do I update my contact details?

You can update your **phone number** and **birthday** yourself on the Profile tab. For name or email changes, just email Matt at &lt;matt.parker@indimortgage.ca>.

## Where do I see my balance and activity?

The **Rewards Bank** tab shows your available points, lifetime earnings, redeemed points, and a full history of how every point was earned or spent.

## Where do I see my referrals?

The portal shows each referral you've submitted and where it stands — from introduction through to funded. You'll also get an email when Matt connects with your referral (and your 10 points land) and again when their mortgage funds (90 more points).

## Who do I contact with questions?

Email Matt directly at &lt;matt.parker@indimortgage.ca> — he personally looks after every member. You can also use the chat bubble in the portal.
