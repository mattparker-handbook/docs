*Matt's Mortgage Money is the client appreciation and referral rewards program from Matt Parker, mortgage broker with indi Mortgage. Every point is worth \$1 CAD, and points never expire.*

## The short version

- **Refer someone to Matt** — you earn **10 points (\$10)** the moment Matt connects with them.
- **Their mortgage funds** — you earn another **90 points (\$90)**. That's **100 points (\$100) per funded referral**.
- **Redeem anytime** — once you have at least 10 points, trade them for digital gift cards from dozens of popular brands, delivered by email.
- **Monthly draw** — every member is automatically entered in a monthly draw for **100 bonus points (\$100)**.
- **Milestone bonuses** — unlock cash bonuses as your funded referrals add up: \$100 at 3, \$250 at 5, and \$500 at 10.

## Where everything lives

Your personal rewards portal at [rewards.mattparker.ca](https://rewards.mattparker.ca) shows your points balance, your referrals and their progress, your draw entries, milestone progress, and the gift card catalogue. There's no password — you sign in with a personal link sent to your email.

## What points are worth

1 point = \$1 CAD. Points never expire and there is no limit to how many you can earn or save.
