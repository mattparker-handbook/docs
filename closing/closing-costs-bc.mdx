Your down payment is only part of the cash needed to complete a purchase. Build a line-by-line closing budget early so legal costs, taxes, adjustments and moving expenses do not become a surprise.

## Start with a personalized estimate

Some handouts use a percentage of the price as a rough planning buffer. That is not a reliable final quote: tax exemptions, property type, lender requirements and transaction details can change the result considerably. Refinancing has a different cost picture from buying. Ask Matt and your legal professional which items apply.

## Legal fees, registration and closing adjustments

Ask your BC lawyer or notary for fees, taxes and disbursements, including searches, registration and any title-insurance premium. The statement of adjustments may include property taxes, strata fees or other prepaid items shared between buyer and seller. Mortgage payout penalties or discharge costs may matter when selling or refinancing.

- Obtain an all-in estimate rather than relying on old brochure fee ranges.
- Ask when final figures will be available.
- Confirm the amount and form of cleared funds needed for the closing appointment.

## Property transfer tax and possible GST

BC property transfer tax is generally based on fair market value when the interest is registered, unless an exemption applies. First-time buyer and newly built home exemptions have detailed eligibility conditions; being a first-time buyer does not automatically remove the tax. New or substantially renovated housing may raise GST and rebate questions. Have your legal or tax professional confirm the current rules for the specific transaction.

## Inspection, appraisal and insurance

Budget for an inspection if chosen, an appraisal if required, and property insurance. A strata owner may still need their own coverage despite the building’s policy. Mortgage default insurance protects the lender; eligible premiums may be added to the mortgage, but this does not mean every closing expense can be financed.

## Keep practical costs and a reserve in the plan

Include utility setup, moving, storage, strata move fees, immediate maintenance and any overlap between homes. Bridge financing adds its own interest and fees. Do not spend money set aside for closing because the purchase deposit has already been paid.

- Keep down payment, closing costs and emergency savings as separate budget lines.
- Ask whether any fee is paid before completion.
- Independently verify changed wire or payment instructions by phone.
- Tell Matt early if funds are tighter than expected so options can be reviewed before deadlines.
