## Why Partner With Us

Working with the Matthew Parker Group means your clients receive specialist-level mortgage advice backed by deep BC market knowledge and real lender relationships. Here's what that means in practice:

- **Speed of pre-approval** — We prioritize referred clients and aim to deliver a lender-ready pre-approval assessment within 24–48 hours of receiving a complete application package, so you're never left waiting when a deal moves fast.
- **Broad lender access** — As a mortgage broker, we have access to major banks, monoline lenders, credit unions, and alternative lenders. We match each client to the lender and product that fits their profile, not just the closest rate.
- **Complex deal expertise** — Self-employed borrowers, new-to-Canada clients, rental portfolios, and non-traditional income profiles are where broker expertise earns its value. We handle deals that branch advisors often can't.
- **BC market knowledge** — Strata financing, foreign buyer rules, BC property transfer tax, leasehold properties, and CMHC restrictions specific to BC are all part of our daily practice. Your clients get advice grounded in the province they're buying in.
- **Dedicated partner line** — Referral partners receive a direct contact channel. You won't be routed through a general intake queue when you have a time-sensitive question about a client's deal.
