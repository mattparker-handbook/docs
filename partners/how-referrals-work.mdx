Referring a client to the Matthew Parker Group is designed to be frictionless for you and seamless for your client. The process below is the same whether you're a Realtor managing a live offer or a Financial Planner proactively connecting a client before they begin their property search. Each stage has a clear owner, a defined timeline, and a built-in communication touchpoint so you always know where things stand.

## The Referral Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Introduction & Warm Handoff">
    Reach out by email or phone to introduce your client. A brief note with the client's name, contact information, general deal scenario, and your relationship to them is all we need to get started. We'll acknowledge the referral within one business day and take it from there. A warm email introduction — where you copy both us and your client on the same message — delivers the best first impression and the smoothest handoff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client Intake (24-Hour Contact)">
    Once the referral lands with us, we contact your client within 24 business hours. Our initial intake conversation covers their purchase goals, timeline, financial profile, and any deal-specific circumstances. We do this before asking for documents so the client feels heard first — not processed. You'll receive a brief confirmation once we've made contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pre-Approval Assessment">
    After intake, we collect the supporting documentation and run a full lender-ready assessment. For straightforward T4-employed files, this typically takes 24–48 hours. For complex files (self-employed, rental portfolios, new-to-Canada), allow 3–5 business days. The output is a clear pre-approval letter your client can present with confidence, along with a rate hold where applicable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You Stay Informed — Partner Updates at Key Milestones">
    With your client's consent, we keep you updated at the milestones that matter most to your work: pre-approval confirmed, lender submission, conditional approval, and commitment letter issued. For Realtors, this means you know exactly what your client is approved for before you write an offer. For Financial Planners, it means the mortgage fits the plan you've built — no surprises at funding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commitment & Closing">
    Once an offer is accepted and a lender commitment is issued, we work directly with the solicitor to ensure all mortgage conditions are satisfied and funds are in place for the closing date. We flag any title, strata, or property-specific issues that could affect mortgage approval as early as possible. You'll be notified when the commitment is unconditional and again when the file is sent to the lawyer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-Closing Follow-Up">
    Closing isn't the end of the relationship. We follow up with your client after closing to confirm everything went smoothly and to set a mortgage review reminder at the appropriate renewal or rate-change window. For Financial Planners, we can share a mortgage summary to integrate into your client's updated financial plan. Clients who feel well-served refer others — and so do the partners who sent them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What We Need From You

We keep the referral handoff as simple as possible. At minimum, we need:

- **Client name** and preferred contact method (phone or email)
- **Approximate purchase price range** or deal scenario (if known)
- **Timing** — actively searching, offer pending, or planning ahead
- **Any context we should know** — e.g., self-employed, recent job change, investment property, prior financing issues

You don't need to pre-qualify your client or gather documents. That's our job. The more context you can share, the better we can prepare for the first conversation — but even a first name and phone number gets the process started.

## What Your Client Can Expect

From the moment we receive your introduction, your client will experience:

- **A prompt, professional first contact** — no automated queues, no delays
- **A judgment-free intake conversation** focused on their goals and situation
- **BC-specific mortgage advice** that accounts for the provincial rules and lenders that actually serve this market
- **Clear explanations** at every stage — we translate mortgage complexity into plain language without being condescending
- **Realistic expectations** — we tell clients what's achievable and what isn't, and we never oversell an approval we can't deliver

<Tip>
  A **warm email introduction** — where you write a short note and copy both your client and our team on the same email — consistently produces better outcomes than a cold referral where the client receives a call they aren't expecting. Something as simple as *"Hi [client name], I'd like to connect you with Matthew Parker Group — they handle all my clients' mortgage financing and I trust them completely. I've copied them here so they can reach out directly"* sets the right tone and dramatically increases intake conversion.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Client Confidentiality and Partner Updates**

  We treat all client information with strict confidentiality in accordance with applicable federal and provincial privacy legislation (PIPEDA and BC PIPA). Partner updates — including pre-approval status, lender submission confirmation, and commitment milestones — are shared with the referring partner only with the client's explicit consent, which we obtain during the intake conversation. If a client prefers that their financial details remain private from the referring party, we respect that and will simply confirm that the file is progressing without sharing specifics.
</Note>
