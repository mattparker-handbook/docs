Use this matrix to quickly assess whether a client is likely to qualify before making a referral — and to set accurate expectations before they begin their property search. This is a working reference tool, not a substitute for a full mortgage assessment. When in doubt, refer early: we can tell you within one conversation whether a deal is fundable and through which lender channel.

## Borrower Profile Qualification Matrix

| Borrower Type | Min Down Payment | Income Verification | Stress Test Rate | Typical Lender |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Employed (T4) — Purchase Price Under $500K | 5% (insured) | NOA + T4 + employment letter | Greater of contract rate + 2% or 5.25% | Bank / Monoline |
| Employed (T4) — Purchase Price $500K–$1.49M | 5% on first $500K + 10% on remainder (insured) | NOA + T4 + employment letter | Greater of contract rate + 2% or 5.25% | Bank / Monoline |
| Employed (T4) — Purchase Price $1.5M+ | 20% (conventional, uninsured) | NOA + T4 + employment letter | Greater of contract rate + 2% or 5.25% | Bank / Monoline |
| Self-Employed (Stated Income / Alt-A) | 10–20% | 2-yr avg net income + bank statements | Greater of contract rate + 2% or 5.25% | Alt / B Lender |
| Self-Employed (Traditional / Fully Disclosed) | 5–20% | T1 Generals + NOA (2 years) | Greater of contract rate + 2% or 5.25% | Bank / Monoline |
| New to Canada (Permanent Resident) | 5% | Employment letter + international credit or 12-mo. history | Greater of contract rate + 2% or 5.25% | Select Banks / CMHC |
| New to Canada (Temporary Resident / Work Permit) | 35% | Employment letter + work permit | Varies by lender | Select Banks |
| Investment / Rental Property | 20% (uninsured only) | T4/T1 + rental income (50–100% offset) | Greater of contract rate + 2% or 5.25% | Bank / Monoline |
| Retired / Pension Income | 5–20% | Pension statement + NOA | Greater of contract rate + 2% or 5.25% | Bank / Monoline |

<Note>
  **December 2024 Rule Change — Insured Mortgage Cap:** Effective December 15, 2024, the federal government raised the maximum purchase price eligible for insured mortgage financing from $1,000,000 to **$1,500,000**. Purchases between $500,000 and $1,499,999 can now qualify with less than 20% down (5% on the first $500K + 10% on the remainder), subject to CMHC/insurer approval. Purchases at $1,500,000 or above still require a minimum 20% down payment and are uninsured. The down payment tiers in the table above reflect these current rules.
</Note>

## Quick Approval Signals

These are the green flags that make a file straightforward to place with an A lender at competitive rates. When your client checks most of these boxes, the mortgage process is likely to be fast and clean:

- **2+ years of stable employment** with the same employer or in the same industry
- **Credit score of 680 or higher** (720+ opens access to the full range of products and best rates)
- **GDS ratio under 39%** and **TDS ratio under 44%** at the current stress test rate
- **Down payment fully sourced** — no undisclosed borrowed funds; gifted funds with a signed gift letter
- **No recent bankruptcy or consumer proposal** — discharged bankruptcy must typically be 2+ years old; consumer proposal fully paid and 2+ years discharged
- **No active collections** or significant outstanding judgements on title
- **Consistent income** — year-over-year income stable or increasing, not declining

## Watch-Out Signals

These aren't automatic declines, but they require a more detailed review and may route the file to an alternative or B lender. Flag these when making a referral so we can prepare appropriately:

- **Recent bankruptcy or consumer proposal** — timing of discharge and rebuilt credit history are critical
- **Variable or seasonal income** — commission-only, tips, or gig income with less than 2 years of history
- **Multiple properties** — each additional property increases complexity; TDS management becomes the primary concern
- **Short employment tenure** — less than 90 days with a current employer, or recently transitioned to self-employment
- **High revolving debt** — credit card balances near the limit suppress credit scores and inflate TDS ratios
- **Non-arms-length transactions** — purchases from family members or private party transactions require additional lender scrutiny
- **Strata properties with deficiencies** — pending special levies, depreciation report concerns, or inadequate contingency funds can affect mortgage eligibility regardless of borrower strength
- **Leasehold properties** — not all lenders will finance leasehold; the lease term and ground lease terms matter

<Note>
  This matrix is a professional reference guide intended to support preliminary client conversations. Actual qualification depends on a complete mortgage application, full document review, lender credit policy at time of submission, and property-specific factors. A client who appears to qualify based on this matrix may not qualify with a specific lender — and a client who appears borderline may qualify through a different channel. When in doubt, contact us before telling your client what they can or cannot afford.
</Note>
